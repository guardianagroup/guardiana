GUARDIANA for Windows
=====================

Guardiana shows you which services your AI talks to and, with Home Mode, every
device on your Wi-Fi: it explains each one in a sentence and cuts only what you
decide. Everything happens on this machine. No account, no server, zero
telemetry.

What the installer did
----------------------
- Copied the program to "Program Files\Guardiana".
- Registered the "Guardiana" service, which starts with Windows.
- Added the "Guardiana" and "Guardiana (menu)" shortcuts to the Start menu.
- It did not change this machine's DNS: you decide that in the panel.
- It opened no port to the network. Only Home Mode, when you turn it on, opens
  ports on your home Wi-Fi; turning it off closes them.

After installing
----------------
Start menu > Guardiana. The panel opens. At the top you will see a notice,
"This PC does not go through Guardiana yet": press the button and the PC's DNS
points at Guardiana, keeping your current one as the backup in case Guardiana
stops. From then on you will see the queries. Cutting one specific name works
from the first minute; wide cuts, by category or for the whole home, wait until
Guardiana has been watching that device for 24 hours.

It is seven days with everything and no card, Home Mode included. After that
you need a subscription; what was written down stays on your disk, whether you
pay or not.

Uninstalling
------------
Settings > Apps > Guardiana > Uninstall. On uninstall, Guardiana turns Home
Mode off, removes its firewall rule and puts the DNS back as it was. The
statement stays in C:\ProgramData\Guardiana; delete it by hand if you do not
want it.

Checking the installation
-------------------------
"Guardiana (menu)" > the check option, or in a command window:
    "C:\Program Files\Guardiana\guardiana.exe" verify

Before installing: check the hash of the .msi file and compare it with
SHA256SUMS and with the public ledger (ledger.jsonl). How to do it, step by
step, in VERIFY.md on the website.

What Guardiana does not do is in WHAT_IT_DOES_NOT_DO.md. Read it.
