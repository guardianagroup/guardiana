GUARDIANA for Windows

GUARDIANA shows you which services your AI talks to and, with Home Mode, every device on your Wi-Fi: it explains each one in a sentence and blocks only what you decide. Everything happens on this PC. No account, no server of ours, zero telemetry.

What this installer will do
- Copy the program to “Program Files\Guardiana”.
- Register the “Guardiana” service, which starts with Windows.
- Add the “Guardiana” shortcut, which opens the panel in your browser, to the Start menu and the desktop, and the “Guardiana (menu)” shortcut to the Start menu.
- Make GUARDIANA this PC's DNS, over IPv6 too, so it starts watching from the first minute. It first keeps an exact copy of your setting; if the service stops, the PC gets its previous DNS back, and uninstalling leaves it exactly as it was.

What it will NOT do
- It does not read what you send or receive: only the service name, the time and the device.
- It opens no port to the network. Only Home Mode, when you turn it on, opens ports on your home Wi-Fi; turning it off closes them.

After installing
The panel opens when the installer finishes and within a minute you will see this PC's queries. To put the DNS back as it was without uninstalling: panel, “Status”. Blocking one specific name works from the first minute; broad blocks, by category or for the whole home, wait until GUARDIANA has been watching that device for 24 hours.

Seven days with every feature, no card needed; after that you need a subscription. What was written down stays on your disk, whether you pay or not.
