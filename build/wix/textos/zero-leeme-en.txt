GUARDIANA ZERO for Windows
==========================

GUARDIANA ZERO is a browser of its own: as you browse, you see at once which
companies each page tries to call behind your back and which ones are cut off,
with their country and the reason. Everything happens on this PC. No account,
no server of ours, zero telemetry.

What the installer did
----------------------
- It copied the program to AppData\Local\Programs\GUARDIANA ZERO, in your
  user folder. It did not ask for an administrator password.
- It added the “GUARDIANA ZERO” shortcut to the desktop and the Start menu.
- It changed nothing in Windows or in your other browsers.

Where your data is
------------------
In AppData\Local\GUARDIANA ZERO: your settings, your favourites, your marked
data, each day's figures and the receipts. Only on this PC. They stay even if
you uninstall; delete them by hand if you do not want them.

Updating
--------
Download the new installer from guardianagroup.com/en/zero.html and open it: it
replaces the previous version in place. If GUARDIANA ZERO was open, it closes
by itself (saving everything) and the new version opens at the end.

Uninstalling
------------
Settings > Apps > GUARDIANA ZERO > Uninstall. It removes the program and the
shortcuts; your data stays.

Checking what you installed
---------------------------
The SHA-256 hash of the installer and of the program, and their signature, are
on guardianagroup.com/en/zero.html and in the public ledger
github.com/guardianagroup/guardiana/blob/main/ledger.jsonl, written before the
download was published.
